Skill Development & Growth

Gained in this session:

  • Taxonomic Nomenclature Proficiency: Mastering the etymological roots of binomial names allows the learner to immediately categorise a pathogen's potential risks during the initial logging of isolates in their workplace LIMS.
  • Phylogenetic Tree Interpretation: The learner develops the ability to interpret genetic distance from sequence similarity matrices, enabling them to justify the relatedness of isolates during workplace contamination investigations.
  • Hazard Group Classification: By applying the 3-domain system and HSE guidance, the learner can independently determine the required containment level (CL2/CL3) for specific microbial groups in a regulated laboratory.

To develop for progression:

  • MALDI-TOF Database Curation: Future laboratory leaders must be able to validate and update internal taxonomic libraries to ensure compliance with ISO 17025 standards and maintain identification accuracy.
  • Genomic Epidemiology Modelling: As UK diagnostics move toward genotypic identification, mastering WGS is essential for senior roles in public health surveillance to distinguish between closely related pathogenic strains.
  • Strategic AMR Surveillance Frameworks: This skill is required to lead national antimicrobial resistance tracking initiatives, connecting local laboratory data to global trends in line with the UK's National Action Plan.
